# 02 — The evidence base: what is proven, what is still faith

The program's honesty is its crown asset; this file applies that honesty to the program
itself.

## Proven (Observed, HIGH — pre-registered, instrumented, replicated where stated)

1. **The apparatus was not worth its price.** Real-terrain premiums +17.6/+26.2/+35.4 min
   per change (B1/B3/B2), multipliers 2.07×/2.47×/5.72×, the worst number on fully repaired
   machinery. Structural, not bug-inflated: every cost-reduction hypothesis in the series
   (artifacts, renderer, prose volume, OpenSpec depth, composites, effort) was individually
   tested and falsified. Killing it was evidence-driven, not fashion.
2. **The lean residue is cheap on real terrain.** On-clause re-runs: B1 1.15×/+2.5 min,
   B3 1.05×/+54 s. The read-and-stop phase costs 3.6–7.3 min; the post-approval phase ran
   *shorter than the plain arm's whole run* both times. The premium tracks the spec gate
   almost perfectly (+0.9 gate-optional / +2.5 / +15.6 gate-owed).
3. **The stop changes shipped software.** Five catches across the program: T4 graphemes,
   B3 label-fallback override (plain silently shipped the rejected default), B2 posture
   crossing surfaced pre-code with alternatives, B2-lean coded-reason override, B3-lean
   guard-only override refusing ~343 LOC of approved-yesterday machinery. Three of those
   are the operator contradicting **his own pre-registered answer** — the single most
   replicated finding of the program, and the one no mechanized harness can reproduce.
4. **Stop design changes outcomes.** "Approve this plan" got ratification; "pick a rung,
   here is what each costs" got two overrides from the same human on the same tasks one day
   apart. This is the program's most product-relevant discovery (and cuts both ways — 06).
5. **The evidence pass is not load-bearing.** Plain discovered every scored convention
   unaided across the arena; each arm (plain, apparatus, lean) also missed ≥1 code-provable
   cause another arm found. Reading mandates buy nothing measurable; judgment quality does.
6. **The runtime holds under abuse.** EA-X4 baseline 60% → 100%/zero-corruption post-V3,
   re-validated; the B3-class duplicate-decision defects were fixed and then validated
   under live fire (B2: zero duplicates, both stops through the runtime).

## Measured but fragile (Observed result, LOW-to-MEDIUM external validity)

- **n = 3 tasks, 1 operator, 1 codebase, 1 model family.** Every governed-value number
  shares one adjudicator, who is also the author, answering stops about tasks he wrote on
  a repo he knows. The convergence findings are robust to this; the *catch* findings are
  not — an operator inclined to override will override (Inferred, HIGH).
- **B2-lean is off-clause** (`xhigh` vs the frozen `high`); its 3.08× is an upper bound and
  the hardest-task ≤20-min criterion is formally unresolved until the owed re-run.
- **Second-exposure effects** are uncontrolled: every lean row ran on a task the operator
  had answered 24h earlier. humanWait comparisons were correctly excluded, but option
  choices may be anchored (Assumption, MEDIUM — would soften finding 4, not reverse it).
- **20 adjudication rows were never Y/N'd**; the divergence-rate headline the program
  designed for was never computed.

## Never measured at all (the holes that matter)

1. **plain+ask.** A one-sentence standing instruction — "ask before deciding anything
   material" — was pre-registered as C-C's cheap alternative and **never ran on real
   terrain**. It is the obvious rival to a 1.05–1.15× stop: if it catches the same
   overrides at ~1.0×, the runtime/Decision Center apparatus loses most of its measured
   defense and CHAOS's moat shrinks to durability/resumability. This is the single most
   important unrun experiment in the program (Inferred, HIGH).
2. **H1–H4** (synthetic arena, planted answer key): calibrated K-hit rates never scored.
3. **The record→future-stop loop end-to-end.** B2 exercised it in one direction (ADR
   recorded at init → caught at run). No experiment has yet shown a record written by a
   *previous lean run* catching a later contradiction — the mechanism the whole product
   story now rests on (Observed gap, HIGH).
4. **Prose regrowth without ceilings.** L-3 was falsified 3/3 (records 94–109 lines vs a
   40-line target) *while ceilings existed as prose*. All ceilings are now removed. There
   is no measurement of what records look like five changes from now (06).
5. **Anyone else.** No second operator, team, or cold-start user has ever run the lean
   core. Every DX claim in 04 is therefore Inferred at best.

## Methodological verdict

As an internal research program: exemplary — pre-registration, frozen prompts, independent
instrumentation, archive-before-revert, published falsifications of its own predictions
(P1 wrong twice, in opposite directions across eras). As external evidence: a well-run
n=1-operator case study. The program earns the right to say "*this* worked *here*, measured
honestly" — and nothing stronger. That sentence is still more than almost any competing
tool can say (05).
